How to interpret the data: The dashboard is based on Moodle audit logs from two institutions. Wayamba University records after 30 September 2024 have been excluded to ensure that only activities within my employment period are represented. The available Wayamba log evidence covers February 2022 to September 2024, while BCI Campus records cover October 2024 to October 2026. My Moodle administration experience began in 2020. The figures represent recorded Moodle events rather than unique users, courses, working hours or individually performed tasks. Some events may result from bulk operations or automated system processes. Activity categories are grouped using Moodle event labels and components to provide a practical overview of administration activities. At BCI Campus, my responsibilities focus on front-end and application-level Moodle administration, academic support and LMS operations. Server-side infrastructure is managed separately by the technical team.
Prepared from user-supplied Moodle log exports. No user names, IP addresses, descriptions or individual-level records are displayed. Obtain institutional approval before publishing institutional data or branding. The dashboard is a static snapshot and is not connected to live Moodle systems.
